## Definition of done

A chapter is not “done” because every Topic has six sections.

It is done when:
- ontology is exact;
- ownership is exact;
- semantic depth passes;
- neighboring concepts are distinguishable;
- evidence and uncertainty are correctly represented;
- relationships are explicit where useful;
- retrieval variants are robust;
- template signatures are absent;
- System 1 remains uncontaminated by downstream sales application;
- final validation records all gates.

## Strategic target

The final system should function simultaneously as:

1. a human psychological encyclopedia;
2. a canonical ontology;
3. a machine-retrievable knowledge base;
4. a typed psychological relationship graph;
5. a scientific evidence map;
6. infrastructure for downstream intelligence systems.

The project should optimize for **knowledge completeness and reasoning quality**, not word count.
