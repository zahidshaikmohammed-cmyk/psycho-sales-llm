# PSYCHO SALES — MASTER SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

## Status

**DRAFT — GLOBAL AUDIT REQUIRED BEFORE LOCK**

## Design rule

The system sequence should follow the psychological and commercial journey of selling while preserving canonical ownership.

A system may apply knowledge from an earlier system, but it must not silently re-own the earlier system's constructs.

---

## SYSTEM 1 — HUMAN PSYCHOLOGY

**Domain:** The general psychological mechanisms underlying human thought, emotion, motivation, perception, learning, judgment, decision-making, behavior, and social functioning.

**Role:** Foundational psychology layer for every later system.

---

## SYSTEM 2 — COLD CALLING PSYCHOLOGY

**Domain:** The psychology of initiating unsolicited or minimally anticipated sales contact.

**Role:** Owns the psychological dynamics specific to the beginning of cold outreach rather than general communication.

---

## SYSTEM 3 — COMMUNICATION PSYCHOLOGY

**Domain:** The psychological processes governing how people encode, transmit, interpret, and respond to information in interpersonal communication.

**Role:** Owns communication mechanisms used by later sales systems without re-owning their domain-specific applications.

---

## SYSTEM 4 — PERSUASION PSYCHOLOGY

**Domain:** The psychological processes through which messages, sources, contexts, and social forces influence attitudes, judgments, intentions, and behavior.

**Role:** Owns persuasion-specific mechanisms, processing, resistance, influence, and outcomes.

---

## SYSTEM 5 — NEGOTIATION PSYCHOLOGY

**Domain:** The psychological processes involved when parties with partially aligned and partially competing interests exchange information, make offers, manage conflict, and seek agreement.

**Role:** Owns negotiation-specific preparation, interaction, strategy, tactics, ethics, outcomes, and scientific foundations.

---

## SYSTEM 6 — BUYER PSYCHOLOGY

**Domain:** The psychological processes specific to buying: buying behavior, motivation, needs, purchase decisions, perceived purchase risk, satisfaction, and post-purchase processes.

**Role:** Owns the buyer-side purchasing domain without re-owning general human psychology, persuasion, or negotiation.

---

## SYSTEM 7 — OBJECTION & RESISTANCE PSYCHOLOGY

**Domain:** The psychological mechanisms behind resistance, objections, hesitation, rejection, defensiveness, and barriers that arise when a buyer does not accept or proceed with a sales proposition.

**Role:** Owns sales-objection and sales-resistance phenomena without re-owning general persuasion resistance.

---

## SYSTEM 8 — RELATIONSHIP PSYCHOLOGY

**Domain:** The psychological development, maintenance, deterioration, and repair of ongoing buyer–seller relationships.

**Role:** Owns long-term relational dynamics specific to sustained commercial relationships.

---

## SYSTEM 9 — SALES PERFORMANCE PSYCHOLOGY

**Domain:** The psychology of the salesperson's own performance: motivation, self-regulation, confidence, stress, resilience, habits, attention, discipline, learning, and performance behavior.

**Role:** Owns the salesperson-as-performer rather than the buyer or the sales interaction itself.

---

## SYSTEM 10 — SCIENTIFIC THEORIES, MODELS & FRAMEWORKS

**Domain:** Cross-system theories, models, frameworks, and formal explanatory structures used to understand the psychological phenomena represented throughout PSYCHO SALES.

**Role:** Provides a scientific synthesis layer without becoming a duplicate encyclopedia of every underlying psychological construct.

---

## SYSTEM 11 — PSYCHO SALES INTELLIGENCE

**Domain:** The integrated reasoning layer that connects the canonical knowledge of Systems 1–10 into structured sales intelligence.

**Role:** Defines how the knowledge system reasons across concepts, detects relationships, constructs explanations, and supports practical sales intelligence without changing canonical ownership.

---

# Global architectural audit

## 1. Foundational ownership

System 1 is the general psychological foundation.

Later systems must not create duplicate owners for general constructs merely by adding a sales-related prefix.

**PASS as a design rule.**

## 2. Sales-process progression

The architecture moves from:
human → contact → communication → persuasion → negotiation → buyer → resistance → relationship → salesperson performance → scientific synthesis → integrated intelligence.

This creates a usable progression from foundational psychology to applied sales intelligence.

**PASS.**

## 3. Buyer/seller separation

System 6 focuses on the buyer.

System 9 focuses on the salesperson.

System 8 focuses on the ongoing relationship.

This prevents the buyer, seller, and relationship perspectives from collapsing into one broad sales chapter.

**PASS.**

## 4. Application versus ownership

A later system may explain how an earlier construct operates in its domain, but should not automatically create a second canonical owner.

Example:
- System 4 owns persuasion.
- System 6 may explain how persuasion is experienced by buyers.
- System 6 does not therefore own persuasion.

**PASS.**

## 5. Objection/resistance boundary

System 4 owns general persuasion resistance.

System 7 must own the sales-specific phenomenon of objections and resistance occurring within sales interactions.

The distinction must be explicit when the ontology is built.

**CONDITIONAL PASS — requires topic-level audit.**

## 6. Relationship boundary

System 8 must not become a duplicate of general social psychology or interpersonal psychology.

It must own sustained buyer–seller relationship phenomena.

**CONDITIONAL PASS — requires topic-level audit.**

## 7. System 10 boundary

System 10 must not become a second System 1.

It should organize and explain theories, models, and frameworks that provide explanatory structure across the encyclopedia.

**CONDITIONAL PASS — requires ontology design.**

## 8. System 11 boundary

System 11 is not another psychology system.

It is the integration/intelligence layer.

It should not create new psychological owners merely because a concept is useful for reasoning.

**PASS as architectural principle.**

# Locking rule

This master architecture is **not permanently locked yet**.

It becomes eligible for lock only after:
1. each system receives a chapter-level ontology;
2. chapter ownership is audited against all earlier systems;
3. the remaining systems are checked for future ownership conflicts;
4. a final cross-system audit confirms that no major domain has two owners.

Only then should this document be renamed/reclassified as the **FINAL LOCKED MASTER ARCHITECTURE**.
