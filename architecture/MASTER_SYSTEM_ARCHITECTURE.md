# PSYCHO SALES — MASTER SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

## Status

**FINAL LOCKED MASTER ARCHITECTURE — SYSTEM/CHAPTER LEVEL**

**Lock principle:** One Concept = One Canonical Owner.

The architecture defines eleven systems. Each system has a distinct psychological or knowledge role. Later systems may apply earlier concepts but may not silently re-own them.

---

## SYSTEM 1 — HUMAN PSYCHOLOGY

General psychological mechanisms underlying human thought, emotion, motivation, perception, learning, judgment, decision-making, behavior, and social functioning.

## SYSTEM 2 — COLD CALLING PSYCHOLOGY

Psychological mechanisms specific to initiating unsolicited or minimally anticipated sales contact.

## SYSTEM 3 — COMMUNICATION PSYCHOLOGY

Psychological processes governing interpersonal communication, including encoding, expression, reception, interpretation, feedback, and communication breakdown.

## SYSTEM 4 — PERSUASION PSYCHOLOGY

Psychological processes through which messages, sources, contexts, and social forces influence attitudes, judgments, intentions, and behavior.

## SYSTEM 5 — NEGOTIATION PSYCHOLOGY

Psychological processes involved when parties with partially aligned and partially competing interests exchange information, manage conflict, bargain, and seek agreement.

## SYSTEM 6 — BUYER PSYCHOLOGY

Psychological processes specific to buying, including buyer motivation, needs and wants, purchase decisions, perceived purchase risk, satisfaction, and post-purchase processes.

## SYSTEM 7 — OBJECTION & RESISTANCE PSYCHOLOGY

Sales-specific psychological mechanisms behind objections, hesitation, rejection, defensiveness, and barriers to proceeding with a sales proposition.

## SYSTEM 8 — RELATIONSHIP PSYCHOLOGY

Psychological processes governing sustained buyer–seller relationships, including formation, trust, commitment, maintenance, deterioration, repair, loyalty, and exit.

## SYSTEM 9 — SALES PERFORMANCE PSYCHOLOGY

Psychology of the salesperson as a performer, including motivation, self-regulation, confidence, attention, stress, resilience, habits, learning, and performance behavior.

## SYSTEM 10 — SCIENTIFIC THEORIES, MODELS & FRAMEWORKS

Scientific theories, models, frameworks, research methods, and formal explanatory structures used to explain phenomena represented across PSYCHO SALES.

## SYSTEM 11 — PSYCHO SALES INTELLIGENCE

Integrated reasoning over Systems 1–10 for knowledge representation, psychological diagnosis, state modeling, scenario reasoning, evidence handling, retrieval, and sales decision support.

---

# Global ownership law

1. Every canonical concept has one owner.
2. A later system may apply a concept without owning it.
3. A domain-specific phenomenon may have its own owner when it is meaningfully different from the general construct.
4. Scientific models belong to System 10; the constructs they explain retain their canonical owners.
5. System 11 integrates knowledge but does not create new psychological owners.
6. Topic-level audits remain mandatory during ontology construction.

---

# Build sequence

**Ontology → ownership audit → lock → knowledge writing → validation → versioning.**

The system/chapter architecture is now locked. Individual topic ontologies are not automatically locked until their ownership audit is passed.
