# CHAPTER 1.2 — SURGICAL DEPTH AUDIT

**Chapter:** 1.2 — Psychological Science, Research, Measurement, and Evidence  
**Scope:** All 118 canonical Knowledge Objects  
**Audit purpose:** Identify concepts that are structurally valid and technically correct but insufficiently complete for the PSYCHO SALES Permanent Knowledge Object Writing Constitution.

## Audit Standard

This audit does **not** use word count as a quality score. Word count is only a screening signal.

A Knowledge Object passes depth when it provides enough information for a reader or AI to:
1. identify exactly what the concept is;
2. distinguish it from neighboring concepts;
3. understand its mechanism, logic, or structure where applicable;
4. understand important forms, dimensions, assumptions, or conditions where applicable;
5. understand major limitations and boundary conditions;
6. understand what the concept can and cannot establish;
7. locate its relationship to adjacent concepts without ownership duplication;
8. interpret its evidence status correctly;
9. retrieve the concept without needing hidden context.

**Stopping rule:** complete understanding, not a target word count.

- **1.2.111 Informed consent** — deepen information, comprehension, voluntariness, capacity, ongoing consent, exceptions, and undue influence.
- **1.2.113 Privacy** — deepen privacy versus confidentiality/anonymity, contextual expectations, digital research, identifiability, and data minimization.

## Cross-object architectural findings

### 1. Length imbalance is real

The chapter currently contains several objects below ~300 words while some neighboring objects exceed 500–800 words. This is not automatically a defect, but the concentration of short treatment occurs disproportionately in technically dense domains such as causal inference, statistics, psychometrics, open science, and research ethics.

### 2. Structural completeness is not conceptual completeness

All 118 objects currently contain the six required top-level sections and Owns/Defers blocks. That proves structural compliance, not sufficient explanatory depth.

### 3. Neighbor distinction needs a dedicated pass

Particular boundary clusters require explicit contrast testing:

- experience sampling / ecological momentary assessment

### 4. Evidence-status language is structurally present

All audited objects contain an evidence-status treatment. The next pass must check whether the **specific wording** correctly distinguishes:
- definition;
- empirical finding;
- association;
- causal evidence;
- theory/model;
- interpretation/inference;
- contested or unresolved status.

### 5. Sales contamination check

The chapter should remain universal scientific infrastructure. No object should be expanded by adding sales examples merely to increase practical detail. Sales relevance belongs downstream and only where genuinely necessary.
