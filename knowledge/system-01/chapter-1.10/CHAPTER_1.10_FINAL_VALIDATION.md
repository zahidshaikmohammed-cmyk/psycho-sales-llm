## Scientific / Evidence Integrity

The chapter separates normative models from descriptive claims, avoids treating confidence as accuracy, avoids defining all heuristic use as error, and preserves context dependence where effects vary by task, information structure, stakes, or measurement method. Named phenomena are not treated as universal traits or diagnoses. Model assumptions are kept distinct from empirical findings.
