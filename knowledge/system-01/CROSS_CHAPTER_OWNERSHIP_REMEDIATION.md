# SYSTEM 1 — CROSS-CHAPTER OWNERSHIP REMEDIATION

**Status:** REMEDIATION PASS COMPLETED — VALIDATION REQUIRED BEFORE RESEAL

## Scope

This audit covers the canonical Knowledge Objects in:

- Chapter 1.1 — Foundations and Architecture of Human Psychology
- Chapter 1.2 — Psychological Science, Research, Measurement, and Evidence
- Chapter 1.3 — The Dynamics of Human Thinking

The objective is not to eliminate conceptual relationships. The objective is to ensure that related Topics have distinct canonical ownership and cannot plausibly provide competing answers to the same "What is X?" question.

## Remediated Ownership Decisions

### 1.1.3 Mental processes ↔ 1.1.27 Psychological processing

- **1.1.3 Mental processes** owns the broad category of internal psychological activity.
- **1.1.27 Psychological processing** owns transformation, organization, integration, updating, and related operations performed during that activity.
- The distinction is therefore **category vs operation**.

### 1.1.30 Representation ↔ 1.3.8 Mental Representation

- **1.1.30 Representation** owns the foundational psychological concept of internal representation.
- **1.3.8 Mental Representation** owns the role and use of mental representations within thinking.
- The distinction is therefore **general construct vs thinking-specific specialization**.

### 1.2.82 Validity ↔ 1.2.52 Measurement validity

- **1.2.82 Validity** owns the overarching psychometric concept.
- **1.2.51 Construct validity** specializes evidence concerning construct interpretation.
- **1.2.52 Measurement validity** owns the narrower measurement-procedure application.
- The distinction is therefore **general construct vs specialized application**.

### 1.3.4 Cognitive Processing ↔ 1.3.5 Mental Processing ↔ 1.3.7 Information Processing

- **1.3.5 Mental Processing** owns the broad operation-level concept for psychological operations on internally available material.
- **1.3.4 Cognitive Processing** owns the cognition-specific specialization.
- **1.3.7 Information Processing** owns the functional description of information transformation and flow.
- **1.3.68 Information Processing Theory** remains the theoretical framework, not the descriptive construct.
- The distinction is therefore **psychological scope vs cognitive scope vs information-flow description**.

### 1.3.13 Logical Thinking ↔ 1.3.19 Logical Reasoning

- **1.3.13 Logical Thinking** owns logical coherence and consistency as characteristics of thought.
- **1.3.19 Logical Reasoning** owns the inferential process relating premises and conclusions.
- The distinction is therefore **quality/organization of thought vs inferential operation**.

### 1.3.51–1.3.55 Prospective family

- **1.3.51 Future Thinking** owns the broad future-oriented cognitive family.
- **1.3.52 Prospection** owns construction and evaluation of possible futures.
- **1.3.53 Prediction** owns expectation that a specified future outcome will occur.
- **1.3.54 Forecasting** owns structured future estimation with an explicit horizon, information base, or method.
- **1.3.55 Anticipation** owns present preparation based on an expected or possible future condition.

### 1.3.63–1.3.66 Inference/output family

- **1.3.63 Inference** owns the deriving operation.
- **1.3.64 Conclusion Formation** owns settling on a proposition as a cognitive output.
- **1.3.65 Explanation** owns construction/evaluation of accounts of how or why something occurs.
- **1.3.66 Hypothesis Generation** owns production of candidate propositions for evaluation.
