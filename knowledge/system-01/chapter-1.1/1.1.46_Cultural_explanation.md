# 1.1.46 — Cultural Explanation

## 1. Purpose
Cultural explanation recognizes that shared meanings, practices, values, norms, and institutions can shape psychological functioning.

## 2. Definition
A cultural explanation accounts for psychological phenomena through culturally shared systems of meaning, practice, values, norms, language, institutions, and learned patterns.

## 3. Core Understanding

Cultural explanations concern shared systems that people learn through participation in communities and institutions. Values, norms, language, practices, categories, and social expectations can shape what is noticed, how events are interpreted, and which behaviors are supported.

Cultural influence can occur through development and everyday interaction. People can also participate in multiple cultural systems, switch between cultural frames, and differ within the same cultural group.

Cultural explanations should avoid treating cultures as homogeneous personalities. A culture is a complex social system containing variation, conflict, change, and multiple identities.

Culture also interacts with biology, individual psychology, and immediate situations. A culturally informed explanation should specify the pathway rather than simply using "culture" as a broad label.

## 6. Key Principles
1. Culture can shape psychological meaning and behavior.
2. Cultural influence is learned and context-dependent.
3. Culture does not determine every individual response.
4. Cultural explanation complements biological and psychological explanation.
