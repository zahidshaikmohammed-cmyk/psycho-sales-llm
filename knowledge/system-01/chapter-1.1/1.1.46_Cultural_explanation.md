# 1.1.46 — Cultural Explanation

## 1. Purpose
Cultural explanation recognizes that shared meanings, practices, values, norms, and institutions can shape psychological functioning.

## 2. Definition
A cultural explanation accounts for psychological phenomena through culturally shared systems of meaning, practice, values, norms, language, institutions, and learned patterns.

## 3. Core Understanding
Culture can influence how people interpret events, express emotion, organize relationships, understand self, and pursue goals. Cultural effects are learned and embedded in social environments.

Culture should not be treated as a single variable that determines behavior. Cultures contain variation, individuals participate in multiple cultural systems, and biological and situational factors also matter.

## 4. Relationship to This Chapter
Cultural explanation is a higher-level contextual explanation linked to Chapter 1.17.

## 5. Boundaries
### Owns
- Cultural explanation as an explanatory level.
### Defers
- Sociocultural psychology → 1.17.

## 6. Key Principles
1. Culture can shape psychological meaning and behavior.
2. Cultural influence is learned and context-dependent.
3. Culture does not determine every individual response.
4. Cultural explanation complements biological and psychological explanation.
