# 1.1.43 — Biological Explanation

## 1. Purpose
Biological explanation identifies how bodily and biological systems contribute to psychological functioning.

## 2. Definition
A biological explanation accounts for psychological phenomena through biological processes, structures, regulation, development, genetic variation, or organismic systems.

## 3. Core Understanding
Biological explanations can involve neural communication, hormones, genes, brain networks, immune processes, and bodily regulation. They can explain constraints, mechanisms, or correlates.

A biological explanation should not imply that psychology is reducible to one brain region or chemical. Most psychological phenomena involve distributed systems and interactions with experience and environment.

## 4. Relationship to This Chapter
It is one of the major explanatory levels and connects to the biological chapter.

## 5. Boundaries
### Owns
- Biological explanation as a general explanatory level.
### Defers
- Detailed biological mechanisms → 1.3.

## 6. Key Principles
1. Biology contributes to psychological functioning.
2. Biological explanation can involve multiple interacting systems.
3. Biological correlates are not automatically complete explanations.
4. Experience and environment can interact with biological processes.
