# 1.1.45 — Social Explanation

## 1. Purpose
Social explanation accounts for psychological functioning through interaction with other people, groups, roles, norms, relationships, and social structures.

## 2. Definition
A social explanation explains psychological phenomena through interpersonal and group processes and the social conditions in which people function.

## 3. Core Understanding
Social factors can influence identity, emotion, cognition, motivation, behavior, and health. Social explanations can concern immediate interaction or broader group structures.

Social influence is not deterministic. People interpret and respond to social conditions differently, and individual characteristics also affect social environments.

## 4. Relationship to This Chapter
Social explanation is one level within the chapter's multilevel architecture and leads into social and cultural psychology.

3. People also shape social environments.
4. Social explanation complements individual and biological explanation.
