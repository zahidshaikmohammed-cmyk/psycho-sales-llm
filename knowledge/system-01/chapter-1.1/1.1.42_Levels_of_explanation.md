# 1.1.42 — Levels of Explanation

## 1. Purpose
Levels of explanation clarify what kind of account is being offered and prevent one explanatory level from being treated as the only legitimate one.

## 2. Definition
A level of explanation is the conceptual scale and type of causal or functional account used to explain a psychological phenomenon.

## 3. Core Understanding
Biological explanations examine mechanisms in nervous, endocrine, genetic, or bodily systems. Psychological explanations examine processes, representations, states, and mechanisms. Social explanations examine interaction and groups. Cultural explanations examine shared meanings and practices.

These explanations can complement one another. A psychological mechanism can have biological implementation and social causes. Choosing a level depends on the question.

## 4. Relationship to This Chapter
This concept connects the chapter's architectural framework to the biopsychosocial and multilevel perspectives.

## 5. Boundaries
### Owns
- Distinction among explanatory levels.
### Defers
- Specific explanations → domain chapters.

## 6. Key Principles
1. Explanations can operate at different levels.
2. One level does not automatically invalidate another.
3. Explanatory level should match the question.
4. Multilevel explanations require clear relationships among levels.
