# 1.1.44 — Psychological Explanation

## 1. Purpose
Psychological explanation describes phenomena through psychological processes, states, representations, mechanisms, and individual differences.

## 2. Definition
A psychological explanation accounts for behavior or mental life using psychological processes or structures and their relationships.

## 3. Core Understanding
Psychological explanations can identify attention, memory, learning, appraisal, motivation, reasoning, or self-regulation as mechanisms. They operate at a level between direct behavior and lower-level biological implementation.

A psychological explanation does not deny biology. It describes a different level of organization and can be linked to biological and environmental explanations.

## 4. Relationship to This Chapter
This is the central explanatory level of System 1 and connects the foundation to every later psychological domain.

## 5. Boundaries
### Owns
- Psychological explanation as an explanatory level.
### Defers
- Specific mechanisms → their canonical chapters.

## 6. Key Principles
1. Psychological explanations describe psychological processes and relations.
2. They can coexist with biological explanations.
3. A psychological label should not be mistaken for a mechanism without evidence.
4. Explanation requires evidence, not plausibility alone.
