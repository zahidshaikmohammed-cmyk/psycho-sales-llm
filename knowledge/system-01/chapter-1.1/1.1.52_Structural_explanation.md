# 1.1.52 — Structural Explanation

## 1. Purpose
Structural explanation describes how psychological components are organized and related.

## 2. Definition
A structural explanation accounts for a phenomenon through the organization, components, and relationships within a psychological system.

## 3. Core Understanding
Structural accounts can describe networks, component relationships, hierarchies, and stable organization. They answer questions about what parts exist and how they are arranged rather than primarily how change occurs.

Structure and process are complementary. Knowing a system's components does not automatically explain its dynamics.

## 4. Relationship to This Chapter
Structural explanation connects psychological organization and systems to later process and mechanism explanations.

## 5. Boundaries
### Owns
- Structural explanation as an architectural level.
### Defers
- Specific structures → relevant chapters.

## 6. Key Principles
1. Structural explanation describes organization.
2. Structure does not automatically explain process.
3. Structural and process accounts can complement each other.
