# PSYCHO SALES — CANONICAL KNOWLEDGE OBJECT CONSTITUTION v2.0

**Status:** CANONICAL UPGRADE STANDARD — LOCKED FOR FUTURE REMEDIATION

## Mission
PSYCHO SALES is not being built as a long book. It is being built as a human-readable, machine-retrievable psychological knowledge system from which later intelligence systems may reason without silently changing the underlying science.

> Complete understanding. Scientific precision. Explicit conceptual ownership. Explicit relationships. Evidence-aware reasoning. Machine retrievability. Human readability. No filler. No artificial depth.

Length is never the target. Understanding is the target.

## Architecture remains authoritative
The locked ontology, Topic identifiers, ownership registry, and chapter architecture remain authoritative unless a formal architecture change is approved. This constitution upgrades the knowledge-object quality standard; it does not authorize casual renaming, deletion, duplication, or expansion.

## Six permanent sections
Every canonical Topic must contain, in this exact order:
1. Purpose
2. Definition
3. Core Understanding
4. Relationship to This Chapter
5. Boundaries
6. Key Principles

These are the stable human-readable shell. They are not sufficient evidence of conceptual completeness.

## Semantic completeness
Each Topic must contain the knowledge necessary for its own subject matter. Applicable dimensions include precise identity; scope; what the concept is not; mechanism or process logic; components or dimensions; forms or variants; antecedents; consequences; temporal dynamics; boundary conditions; context dependence; individual differences; developmental variation; biological relationships; social and cultural relationships; measurement and operationalization; major paradigms; competing explanations; theory/model status; evidence status; uncertainty; limitations; common misconceptions; neighboring concepts; conceptual contrasts; cross-chapter relationships; and retrieval aliases.

Not every Topic needs every dimension. Omission must be semantically justified.

## Conceptual distinction gate
For every Topic, identify the nearest plausible neighboring concepts. The Topic must make the distinctions necessary to prevent a competent reader or model from collapsing those concepts together.

## Mechanism gate
Where a Topic describes a process, mechanism, causal pathway, regulatory system, or functional operation, explain how it operates at the appropriate level. Never invent mechanisms or convert proposed mechanisms into established fact.

## Evidence gate
Preserve the difference among definition, observation, association, causal evidence, theory/model, interpretation, speculation, and unresolved question.

## Uncertainty gate
Uncertainty is knowledge. Where scientific boundaries are unresolved, say so. Do not manufacture certainty or controversy.

## Boundary gate
Every Topic must state what it owns and what it defers. Deferral must be meaningful; generic repeated deferral language is insufficient where important cross-chapter boundaries exist.

## Relationship gate
Important relationships are knowledge. Where relevant, represent influences, prediction, mediation, moderation, constraint, enablement, dependence, temporal order, interaction, competition, dissociation, overlap, and distinction without creating duplicate construct ownership.

## Retrieval gate
A canonical Topic must be retrievable from natural-language variants without hidden context. Where useful, include synonyms, alternate terminology, historical terminology, abbreviations, and common retrieval variants. Aliases never create competing definitions.

## Human-readable depth
Simple language is required. Simple language does not mean shallow knowledge. Foundational Topics may require extensive treatment; narrow Topics may require little. The stopping rule is: remove prose only when removing it does not reduce correct understanding.

## Anti-template law
No Topic may contain generic filler generated merely to satisfy a schema. Audit smells include literal placeholders, identical explanatory paragraphs, identical relationship paragraphs, identical key-principle sets, and generic chapter-membership prose that conveys no Topic-specific information.

## Scientific contamination law
System 1 describes universal psychology. Sales, persuasion, buyer behavior, conversion, negotiation, commercial tactics, or product strategy must not redefine a universal psychological construct. Applications belong downstream.

## Machine composability
The prose corpus is the human-readable layer. Future machine systems should also be able to represent Topic ID, ontology type, owner, aliases, parent/child relations, cross-links, relationship types, evidence status, uncertainty, theory/model status, measurement references, and clinical/application status.

## Seal requirements
A Topic or chapter may be marked LOCKED & SEALED only after ontology match; structural validation; ownership validation; semantic depth audit; neighboring-concept distinction audit; scientific-integrity audit; relationship/retrieval audit; anti-template audit; cross-chapter boundary audit; and a final validation record.

## Versioning
When semantic depth improves without changing the locked ontology, preserve Topic IDs and names, record the revision, increment the knowledge version, and retain the validation trail.

## Final quality law
> The encyclopedia is finished only when the knowledge is complete enough that a competent human and a capable machine can use each Topic without hidden assumptions, while the system remains honest about what psychology does not yet know.
