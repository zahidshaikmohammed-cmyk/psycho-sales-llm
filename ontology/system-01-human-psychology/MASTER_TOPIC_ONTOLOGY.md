# SYSTEM 1 OWNERSHIP RULES

## Rule 1 — General mechanisms live here

If a concept describes a psychological process that applies broadly across human behavior, System 1 is the default canonical owner.

## Rule 2 — Context does not automatically create a new owner

“Buyer motivation,” “sales motivation,” and “negotiation motivation” may describe domain-specific applications. They do not replace general motivation as the foundational construct.

## Rule 3 — Domain phenomena can be narrower than general mechanisms

A cold-call interruption, a buyer objection, or a salesperson performance state can have a distinct owner because each is embedded in a specific commercial situation.

## Rule 4 — Models remain distinct from constructs

A theory or model explaining attention, motivation, decision-making, or behavior is not itself the owner of those constructs.

## Rule 5 — No topic should become a dumping ground

A topic must explain its own concept deeply, then stop when the explanation would become the canonical explanation of a neighboring concept.
